Hi, I'm **Tomáš Hadámek**, a scientist, a creative developer and hobby builder who is not afraid of using his hands and getting them dirty.

The main project topics, besides my work, have been connected to my strong passions for biking, music, acoustics and electronics.

Besides my creative projects, I love photography, dancing and enjoy outdoor sports. 

In the recent years I got drawn to ultra-endurance biking and bike-packing racing and attended some of well known races such as The Silk Road Mountain Race, 1000 Miles Adventure, Loudání, Istraland. 

I have also been an active member and a tour lead of the [Wiener Shotteria](https://schotteria.com/) gravel biking community/project.
