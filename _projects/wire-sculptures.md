## The Goal
The idea of giving something special to people around me, either for their birthday or for some important life milestones, has been in my head for as long as I can remember. At some point, I was drawn to the idea of simply winding a piece of wire into different sculptures with a special meaning or theme for the recipient. I think the time and care invested in a personal gift are far more valuable than buying something ready-made.

## The Projects
The first sculptures I created date back to 2015, when a good friend of mine had her birthday. As we both lived in Norway, I made a small skier. Then a mountain biker for my then girlfriend followed.
A few months later, a good friend, a passionate climber, was leaving Norway, so I made her two climbers on a wall.

Other projects followed throughout the years, but the simple techniques of wound wires remained.

At some point, I realized that a more stable base would be beneficial, so I started using wood and foam board, which also allowed me to imprint important jubilees. To add a bit of pop to the sculptures, I also began using different wire materials. Among others, I created a highliner, an accordionist, and a man holding a polarised electron to celebrate my colleague's PhD defence.
