## Goal
For a long time I have been collecting projects and photos and wanted to have some space to present them at one place. A website was a natural place and was on my to-do list for a while. I wanted a relatively simple website with a few separate pages, namely Projects, Blog, Gallery. 



## The Website and Design
I have been working with GitHub for a while and Jekyll on GitHub Pages seemed to be the most versatile option while providing free webpage hosting. 

I looked up quite a few templates, but non of them was to my liking. I even downloaded a few, but realized that changing them was more complicated than just coding the website myself. 

I was surprised how well it went with a bit of AI help. It was a fun process with a lot of new things to learn. The website is not perfect and will continuously get some updates, but so far I am happy with the result. 

I decided to separate Projects and Blog. They are both cross-linked together. The Project page basically lists all my projects. In the Blog, I post all the project updates. The Gallery is a self standing page with a selection of my pictures.
