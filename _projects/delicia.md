## Goal
After years of playing a small Hohner Concerto I with 48 bass buttons and only 26 treble tones, I realized I need a bigger instrument. I wanted an instrument with 72 bass buttons and low register in the treble. 


## The Instrument 
My first idea was to get a widely available Hohner Concerto III, but I could not get a hold on any affordable instrument in a decent shape. In November 2025 I found this Czechoslovakian Delicia Arlando III from the seventies for 100 eur including shipping so I decided to give it a chance. 

The bellows were in a good shape. The rest of the instrument looks OK. At least, that was what I could see from the picture. 

The reality was a bit different. When the instrument arrived I realized that the body has some damage which was previously fixed. It looks like the instrument was dropped on one of the right bottom corner and glued together. A few more bits were broken/cracked at the bottom of the instrument and so I had to do a bit of wood working before doing anything else. 

While gluing together the body of the instrument, I realized that the wood blocks holding the bass machine were also loose and requited fixing. The original glue and two nails were apparently not enough to hold them in place. 

![Bass mechanics support]({{ "/assets/images/projects/delicia/bass-mechanics-support-1.jpg" | relative_url }})

I also noticed that the celluloid of the bottom cover started to come off so I simply glued it back.

![Celluloid coming off]({{ "/assets/images/projects/delicia/celluloid.jpg" | relative_url }})

When the mechanical part of the instrument was OK, I could start with the reeds.
