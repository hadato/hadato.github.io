## Goal
This project came from a need of a faster bike which would be capable of handling both road rides, gravel biking and easy bike-packing. 

## Project Description
The idea for this project was sitting in my head since 2015, but I never had space to do it. In 2017 I found this beautiful Japanese Nishiki Sri Team on the second hand market in a mint shape. 

The components came from another bike from that era - the Al frame was beaten up, and 3 sizes bigger than I needed, so I trashed it and stripped down the components. 

The biggest challenge was to get some Shimano STIs to work with the original drivetrain. In the end I managed to find vintage Shimano R500 STIs which were in a decent shape so I took them. 

Only a few components were purchased new (stem, handlebars, tubes, one tire, brake pads and cables/cable housings). The rest came with the frame of the other bike. 

When I first sat it on the bike, it was a blast! Sooooooo much fun! I quickly became my to-go-to bike for quite a bit of riding I was doing with other people. It could shred trails, gravel, but was not lost on longer road rides either!

I knew that these monster gravel bikes (very close to original mountain bikes) would be fun, but I never expected this much fun! Since this first project I have rebuilt more than handful of MTBs into monster gravels and everyone who tried them was extremely excited.
