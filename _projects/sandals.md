## The Goal
A few years ago (in 2022?) I could not find barefoot sandals I would be happy with. The available models were either too expensive for what they were, or not really barefoot. Therefore, I wanted to make sandals myself.

## The Project
The idea was simple: creating minimalistic sandals which would be light to pack and still nice to walk in. The true minimalist sandals I found usually contained only the rubber sole and the paracord, but after an experience with plastic/rubber-only sandals, I wanted to have an extra leather layer to prevent slipping and odors. 

I simply purchased the cheapest 4 mm thick soles from https://www.sijemezkuze.cz/en/, 0.8 mm thin soft leather, shoe contact glue and various colored paracords in 4 mm. As I tend to prefer blue, I decided to go for dark blue leather and beige and brown paracords. 

The process was simple. I roughly copied the shape of my foot onto a piece of paper and added a few millimeters to all sides. I then simply cut out the required shapes from the rubber soles. Afterwards, I applied the contact glue to both the leather and the soles, let it set for a few minutes, and then hammered it down to create sufficient pressure. The required holes followed. I decided on a simple design with paracord between my big toe and second toe to make the sandals more stable. By far the biggest challenge was creating stable and small enough paracord endings. I had seen knots hammered flat, but I found them too big and bothersome when walking. Therefore, I melted the pieces of paracord and tried to make a blob, which I then hammered flatter. This somehow worked.

Since their creation, the sandals have held up surprisingly well, although the heavy usage is clearly visible. I only had to re-do one of the knots at the bottom between the toes. On one of the sandals, part of the leather on the heel wore through, so there is only the rubber left. Next time, I will use thicker leather to prevent this from happening.
