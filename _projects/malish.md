## Goal
For a long time I wanted a smaller travel instrument. This small Malish chromatic button accordion meant for kids with a standard Stradella bass seems to be a very nice choice. Only about 2.5 kg - 2 kg lighter than my Honer Concerto I. And look at the color!!!



## History and First Steps
I bought the accordion from Ukraine in 2023 and have been slowly making progress in its restoration. As the instrument is very small, the bass section is limited to two voices only with a lack of lower bass. 

As the original reeds were of a really bad quality and rusted, I decided to buy high quality Tipo A-Mano reeds from Harmonicas, custom made from this accordion. The promise of the better reads is a reduced air consumption. This is especially important for such a small instrument! I also decided to try to fit in a lower octave in the bass - purely for curiosity. Lets see how that goes.
