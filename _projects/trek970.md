## Goal
A goal of this project was to build a relatively budget bike for a friend of mine for commuting, easy terrain and occasional bike-packing. 

## Project Description
While I like modern gravel bikes, they were above the intended budget. The choice was then simple. Choosing a relatively solid old MTB which could easily be rebuild into a monster gravel with minimal budget while keeping the vintage style and usability. The bike was intended for a friend  with a height of 165 cm. 

This turned out to be a bit challenging as most of the older MTBs are relatively long. A choice of a short stem and handlebars with short reach was must. The choice was on a 70 mm 35° stem with Ritchey BioPace gravel bars, in silver color. The original cantilever brakes were in a decent shape, but didn't combine too well with the Shimano Sora STIs. Therefore I donated my vintage XT parallelogram brakes. 

Another challenge was to find a vintage crankset with 104 mm BCD for easy chainring replacement. Luckily I found an second hand Shimano STX RC FC-MC40 in 170 mm. While the 170 mm length is not 100% ideal, it provides more comfort than the original 175 mm crankset. I decided to keep the 3 chainrings for versatility (would not do it again honestly) and replace the worn out cassette by an entry-level 9 speed Sram cassette to match the Sora STIs. 

The tire choice was simple: Continental Race King Protection 2.2" tannwall.

As a bartape I chose See Me from Cycology which nicely complements and livens the bike.
