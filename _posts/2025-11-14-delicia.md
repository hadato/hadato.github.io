The first step to make the instrument at least playable without any weird sounds was to rewax some of the loose reeds. I realized that the low octave bass reeds hold well (because of the extra nails), but reeds in the upper registers got loose. Therefore, I started with them.

Still a lot to go, but now the instrument is playable. Hurray!
