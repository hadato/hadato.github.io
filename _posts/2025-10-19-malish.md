I finally waxed in new Tipo A-Mano reeds from Harmonikas. They have been sitting on my shelf for a while. I decided to only include one set of reeds even though I have the second tremolo reed set ready. If needed, I can always way in the second set, but the single, however, the single reed sounds sounds more pleasant to my ear. 

The treble side is now ready to be played.
